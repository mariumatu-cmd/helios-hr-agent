# Learning, Development, and Tuition Assistance Policy

## 1. Purpose

Helios Systems, Inc. ("Helios") funds learning because the alternative — hiring
every new capability from outside — is slower and more expensive. This policy
sets out what Helios pays for, who is eligible, and how to get approval.

## 2. Eligibility

| Employment type | Learning budget | Tuition assistance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full-time (>= 30 hrs/week) | Full amount per §3 | Eligible after 12 months |
| Part-time (20 – 29 hrs/week) | Pro-rated at 0.5x | Eligible after 18 months |
| Temporary / Intern | Not eligible | Not eligible |
| Contractor | Not eligible | Not eligible |

Employment type is defined in `POL-BEN-001` §2. Employees serving a
performance improvement plan under `POL-PERF-001` remain eligible for the
learning budget but not for tuition assistance, which requires a commitment
period that a PIP makes uncertain for both sides.

## 3. Annual Learning Budget

Each eligible employee has an annual learning budget, allocated by career level
and reset on **1 January**:

| Level | Annual budget |
| --- | --- |
| IC1 – IC2 / equivalent | 1,200 USD |
| IC3 – IC4 / equivalent | 1,800 USD |
| IC5+ and all people managers | 2,500 USD |

### 3.1 What the Budget Covers

- Online courses, subscriptions, and certification exam fees.
- Technical and professional books.
- Conference registration, and the associated travel booked under
  `POL-EXP-001`.
- Professional body membership where relevant to the role.
- Language tuition where the employee works regularly across Helios entities.

### 3.2 What It Does Not Cover

- Hardware, monitors, or peripherals — see `POL-EQUIP-001`.
- Degree programmes — these fall under tuition assistance in §5, which has its
  own, larger budget.
- Coaching or therapy for personal (non-professional) reasons — the Employee
  Assistance Programme under `POL-SAFE-001` §7 covers this at no cost.

### 3.3 Carry-Over

Unused budget does **not** carry into the following year. Helios does not pay
cash in lieu of an unused learning budget.

An employee who joins mid-year receives a pro-rated budget for that year,
calculated as one twelfth of the annual amount for each full month remaining,
including the month of hire.

## 4. Approval

Learning spend up to **500 USD** per item requires manager approval only.
Above that, it requires manager approval and a People Operations
countersignature. Conference attendance always requires manager approval
regardless of cost, because it consumes working time as well as budget.

Approvals are requested through an HR ticket of category `learning`. Approval
must be obtained **before** committing the spend; Helios may decline to
reimburse an unapproved purchase, and routinely does so for non-refundable
conference tickets.

## 5. Tuition Assistance

### 5.1 Scope

Tuition assistance supports part-time study toward an accredited degree or a
substantial professional qualification where the subject is relevant to the
employee's current role or a realistic next role at Helios.

### 5.2 Amount

Helios reimburses up to **5,250 USD per calendar year**, which aligns with the
maximum that is excluded from taxable income under current US rules. Amounts
above this threshold are not offered.

Reimbursement is paid **after** each course or module completes, on evidence of:

- a final grade of **C or better**, or "pass" in a pass/fail course; and
- an itemised receipt showing tuition paid.

Helios does not reimburse application fees, late fees, accommodation, or the
cost of re-sitting a failed course.

### 5.3 Commitment and Repayment

Tuition assistance carries a **12-month commitment period** starting from the
date of each reimbursement. An employee who resigns or is terminated for cause
within that period repays a pro-rated share:

| Months completed since reimbursement | Repayment |
| --- | --- |
| 0 – 3 | 100% |
| 4 – 6 | 75% |
| 7 – 9 | 50% |
| 10 – 12 | 25% |
| 12+ | None |

No repayment is required where employment ends through redundancy, medical
separation, or any termination not for cause. Repayment terms are confirmed in
writing before the first reimbursement is paid.

## 6. Learning Time

Employees may spend up to **4 hours per month** of working time on approved
learning. This time is scheduled with the manager like any other commitment and
is not deducted from PTO under `POL-PTO-001`.

Conference attendance during a working day counts as working time, not PTO.
Travel to a conference on a non-working day does not generate overtime for
non-exempt employees unless work is performed; see `POL-PAY-001` §3.3.

## 7. Certifications

Helios pays for a first attempt at an approved certification exam, and for one
re-sit. Further attempts are at the employee's own cost. Where a certification
requires periodic renewal, renewal fees are charged against the annual learning
budget in the year they fall due.

Certifications obtained at Helios expense belong to the individual, not to
Helios. Nothing in this policy obliges an employee to remain at Helios because
of a certification, except where tuition assistance repayment under §5.3
applies.

## 8. Internal Mobility and Mentoring

Employees with at least 12 months of service may apply for internal roles
without their current manager's permission, and People Operations will not
disclose the application to that manager before an offer stage is reached.

Mentoring is voluntary on both sides. Time spent mentoring is recognised in
performance review under `POL-PERF-001` and does not draw on the learning
budget.

## 9. Records

Learning and tuition records are retained for six years and are visible to the
employee, their manager, and People Operations. They are not shared with
interviewers for internal roles.

## 10. Questions

Raise an HR ticket of category `learning`, or contact your People Operations
partner.
