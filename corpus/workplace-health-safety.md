# Workplace Health, Safety, and Wellbeing Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Helios Systems, Inc. ("Helios") is responsible for the health and safety of its
people while they work, and that responsibility does not stop at the office
door. This policy applies in Helios offices, at approved remote work locations,
and while travelling on Helios business.

Where an employee works remotely, the arrangement itself is governed by
`POL-REMOTE-001`, and internationally by `POL-INTL-001`. This policy governs
the safety of the space, wherever it is.

## 2. Responsibilities

| Who | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| All workers | Work safely, report hazards and incidents, complete assigned safety training |
| Managers | Ensure their team can raise concerns, act on reported hazards, release time for training |
| People Operations | Maintain this policy, triage incident reports, track corrective actions |
| Office leads | Maintain evacuation plans, first aid provision, and equipment checks at each site |

No employee is expected to continue working in conditions they reasonably
believe to be unsafe. Stopping work in that situation is protected under §10
and is never treated as refusal to work.

## 3. Reporting Incidents and Hazards

### 3.1 What to Report

Report any injury, illness arising from work, near miss, or hazard — including
those that caused no harm. Near misses are reported precisely because nothing
happened yet.

### 3.2 How and When

| Severity | Report within | Channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Serious injury, or any hospital treatment | Immediately | Call the office lead, then raise a ticket |
| Injury requiring first aid | 24 hours | HR ticket, category `safety` |
| Near miss or hazard | 3 business days | HR ticket, category `safety` |

Every report is acknowledged within two business days. Reports of a hazard may
be made anonymously; reports of an injury cannot, because they may generate
insurance and statutory obligations that require the individual's details.

### 3.3 Investigation

Incidents involving injury, and near misses with potential for serious harm,
are investigated to identify the cause and a corrective action with a named
owner and a date. Investigation under this policy establishes **what** happened
and how to prevent recurrence. Where conduct is also in question, that is
handled separately under `POL-GRIEV-001`.

## 4. Workstations and Ergonomics

### 4.1 In the Office

Each Helios office provides height-adjustable desks, adjustable seating, and
monitor arms as standard. Office leads arrange an individual workstation
assessment on request, and always following a return from injury-related
absence.

### 4.2 Working from Home

Employees working from home are responsible for maintaining a safe workspace
and must complete the **home workstation self-assessment** during onboarding
(see `POL-ONBOARD-001`) and again after any change of address.

Where the self-assessment identifies a problem, People Operations arranges a
remote ergonomic consultation. Equipment needed to resolve it is provided under
`POL-EQUIP-001`, which sets out what Helios supplies and the applicable
allowances. This policy does not set equipment values; `POL-EQUIP-001` is the
single source for that.

### 4.3 Adjustments

An employee with a disability or health condition may request a workplace
adjustment at any time, through People Operations rather than their manager if
they prefer. Helios does not require a diagnosis to be disclosed to the
manager; it requires only enough information to identify a workable adjustment.

## 5. Work-Related Injury

An employee injured at work is entitled to workers' compensation benefits under
the law of their work state. People Operations initiates the claim once an
injury is reported under §3.

Time absent due to a work-related injury is **not** deducted from the PTO
balance under `POL-PTO-001`. Where absence extends beyond short-term recovery,
`POL-LEAVE-002` (Parental and Medical Leave) governs the leave.

## 6. Illness and Infectious Disease

Employees who are unwell should not attend an office. Employees who are well
enough to work but contagious should work remotely where their role allows,
with their manager's agreement, without this counting against any hybrid
attendance expectation in `POL-REMOTE-001`.

Short-term sickness absence is recorded but is not, by itself, a performance
matter. Helios does not operate an attendance points system.

## 7. Mental Health and the Employee Assistance Programme

Helios provides an Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) to all workers,
including contractors and interns, and including household members. The EAP
offers short-term counselling, legal and financial guidance, and crisis
support.

The EAP is:

- **free** to the user, and not charged against the learning budget in
  `POL-LEARN-001` or any benefits election under `POL-BEN-001`;
- **confidential** — Helios receives aggregate usage statistics only, never
  names or the content of any session;
- **available without manager approval**, and outside working hours.

Using the EAP is not recorded in the personnel file and has no bearing on
performance review or promotion under `POL-PERF-001`.

## 8. Emergency Procedures

Each office publishes evacuation routes, assembly points, and the names of
current first aiders and fire wardens. Evacuation drills are held at least
annually at every site.

On hearing an alarm, leave by the nearest safe exit and go to the assembly
point. Do not use lifts. Do not return for equipment; every device Helios
issues is replaceable and is backed up under `POL-SEC-001`.

## 9. Workplace Violence and Threats

Threats, intimidation, and violence are prohibited and are gross misconduct
under `POL-GRIEV-001` §7.

Where there is an immediate risk to safety, call the local emergency number
first and Helios second. No policy takes precedence over calling for help.

Helios will support an employee experiencing domestic abuse that may follow
them to work, including by changing work location, adjusting hours, or
screening contact, without requiring evidence.

## 10. Protection for Raising Safety Concerns

No worker will be disadvantaged for raising a safety concern in good faith, for
refusing unsafe work, or for participating in a safety investigation.
Retaliation is gross misconduct under `POL-GRIEV-001` §10.

## 11. Travel Safety

Employees travelling on Helios business should register their itinerary in the
travel system. Expense rules for travel are in `POL-EXP-001`; visa, tax, and
duration rules for working abroad are in `POL-INTL-001`.

Helios provides travel medical cover for business travel. An employee who feels
unsafe in a location may end the trip and return, and Helios will cover the
cost of doing so.

## 12. Training and Review

All new starters complete safety induction within 30 days. First aiders and
fire wardens are trained and re-certified on the schedule set by their
certification body. This policy is reviewed annually and after any serious
incident.
