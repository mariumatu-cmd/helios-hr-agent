# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Purpose

Helios Systems supports flexible work arrangements where they are compatible
with the employee's role, team needs, and legal obligations. This policy defines
the available work arrangements, who is eligible for each, and how to request a
change.

Temporary work from a location outside the employee's home country is governed
by `POL-INTL-001` (International Remote Work and Tax Compliance) and requires
additional approvals.

## 2. Work Arrangement Types

Every employee is assigned exactly one of three work arrangements, recorded as
`work_arrangement` in the HR system.

### 2.1 Onsite

The employee works from an assigned Helios office five days per week. Onsite is
required for roles with physical-presence requirements, including hardware lab
engineering, facilities, and IT asset management.

### 2.2 Hybrid

The employee works from an assigned Helios office a minimum of **three (3) days
per week**, with anchor days set by their team. The remaining days may be worked
from an approved remote location within commuting distance.

Hybrid is the default arrangement for employees assigned to an office location.

### 2.3 Remote

The employee has no assigned office and works from a registered home address.
Remote employees are expected to travel to a Helios office for team onsites up
to **four (4) times per year** at company expense, governed by `POL-EXP-001`.

## 3. Eligibility for Fully Remote Status

An employee is eligible to request fully remote status only if **all** of the
following are true:

1. The employee has completed at least **six (6) months** of continuous service
   at Helios.
2. The employee's most recent performance rating is **"Meets Expectations" or
   higher** (see `POL-PERF-001`).
3. The employee is not currently on a performance improvement plan.
4. The role is designated remote-eligible in the job architecture. Roles
   designated `Onsite Required` are not eligible under any circumstances.
5. The employee's registered work location is a US state, or a country, in which
   Helios is a registered employer. As of the effective date these are:
   **United States** (all states except Alaska and Hawaii), **Ireland**,
   **Singapore**, **Canada** (Ontario and British Columbia only), and
   **Portugal**.

Employees in their first six months may still work hybrid; the six-month
requirement applies only to fully remote status.

## 4. Requesting or Changing an Arrangement

1. The employee submits a Work Arrangement Change request in Helios Workday.
2. The direct manager reviews for team-coverage impact and approves or denies.
3. For a change **to fully remote**, the skip-level manager and the HR Business
   Partner must also approve.
4. Approved changes take effect at the start of the next full pay period.
5. Approved changes to the registered work address trigger a payroll and tax
   review, which may take up to **15 business days**.

An employee may request a change no more than twice per calendar year.

## 5. Temporary Remote Work (Domestic)

An employee may work temporarily from a US location other than their registered
address without changing their work arrangement, subject to duration limits:

| Duration | Approval required | Additional requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 – 10 consecutive business days | Manager notification only | None |
| 11 – 20 consecutive business days | Manager written approval | Security review under `POL-SEC-001` §7 |
| 21 – 30 consecutive business days | Manager + HR Business Partner | Security review + payroll notification |
| More than 30 consecutive days in a state where the employee is not registered | Not permitted as temporary work | Requires a permanent work-location change under Section 4 |

The **30-day rule** exists because working more than 30 days in a state creates
state income-tax withholding and, in some states, employer-registration
obligations. Cumulative days in the same state within a rolling 12-month period
count toward the 30-day threshold.

## 6. Temporary Work Outside the Home Country

Working from outside the employee's home country — for any duration — is **not**
covered by Section 5. It requires prior approval under `POL-INTL-001`, which
imposes a separate day limit, tax review, immigration check, and security
review. Booking travel before receiving approval under `POL-INTL-001` is at the
employee's own risk.

## 7. Workspace Requirements

Employees working remotely must maintain a workspace that:

- has a reliable internet connection of at least **25 Mbps download / 5 Mbps
  upload**;
- is private enough to hold confidential conversations without being overheard,
  as required by `POL-SEC-001` §4;
- is free of recognised safety hazards; and
- is located at the address registered in the HR system.

Helios provides a one-time home-office stipend and ongoing equipment support
under `POL-EQUIP-001`.

## 8. Core Collaboration Hours

All employees, regardless of arrangement, must be available during core
collaboration hours of **10:00 – 15:00 in their registered location's local
time zone**, Monday through Friday.

Employees temporarily working from a time zone more than **6 hours** offset from
their team's primary time zone must agree an overlap schedule with their manager
in advance. This applies to both domestic and international temporary work.

## 9. Expectations and Non-Compliance

Remote and hybrid work are conditional privileges, not entitlements. Helios may
modify or revoke an individual's arrangement where there is a documented
performance concern, a change in role requirements, or non-compliance with this
policy.

Working from an unregistered location, or exceeding the limits in Section 5 or
`POL-INTL-001` without approval, may expose both the employee and Helios to tax
and immigration liability and is treated as a policy violation under
`POL-CONDUCT-001`.
