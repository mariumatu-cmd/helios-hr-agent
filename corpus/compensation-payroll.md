# Compensation and Payroll Administration Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how Helios Systems, Inc. ("Helios") pays its people: when
pay is issued, what it is composed of, how errors are corrected, and how pay
changes take effect. It applies to all US-based employees, including part-time
and temporary employees.

Compensation *decisions* — who is promoted, and to what level — are governed by
`POL-PERF-001` (Performance Management and Promotion). This policy covers the
mechanics of paying whatever those decisions produce. Benefits deductions are
defined in `POL-BEN-001` (Benefits and Eligibility). Expense reimbursement is
not compensation and is governed by `POL-EXP-001`.

## 2. Pay Cycle

### 2.1 Paydays

Helios pays semi-monthly: **24 pay periods per year**. Pay periods run from the
1st to the 15th, and from the 16th to the last calendar day of the month.

| Pay period | Payday |
| --- | --- |
| 1st – 15th | 22nd of the same month |
| 16th – end of month | 7th of the following month |

Where a payday falls on a weekend or a company holiday listed in `POL-HOL-001`,
payment is issued on the preceding business day. Helios does not delay payroll
into a holiday.

### 2.2 Payment Method

Direct deposit is the default and is strongly preferred. Employees may split
net pay across at most two accounts. Changes to bank details must be made in
the payroll system and take effect for the **next** pay period; changes
submitted within three business days of a payday will not apply to that payday.

Bank detail changes are a common target for fraud. Payroll will never action a
change requested by email alone; see `POL-SEC-001` for the reporting path if
you receive such a request.

## 3. Components of Pay

### 3.1 Base Salary

Base salary is expressed as an annual figure and divided evenly across the 24
pay periods. Part-time employees are paid a pro-rated base reflecting their
contracted `weekly_hours`.

### 3.2 Variable Pay

| Component | Eligibility | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual performance bonus | Full-time, hired on or before 30 September | March payroll |
| Referral bonus | All employees except the hiring manager and recruiters | Second payday after referee's 90th day |
| On-call stipend | Engineering roles on a published rota | Month following the rota |
| Equity refresh | Per `POL-PERF-001` | Annual cycle |

Bonuses are discretionary unless stated otherwise in a written offer or plan
document, and are not earned until paid.

### 3.3 Overtime and Classification

Employees are classified as exempt or non-exempt under the Fair Labor Standards
Act. Classification is a legal determination based on duties and salary, not a
preference, and cannot be changed by request.

Non-exempt employees are paid **1.5x their regular hourly rate** for hours
worked beyond 40 in a workweek. Overtime must be approved in advance by the
employee's manager, but Helios pays approved and unapproved overtime alike:
working the hours is what creates the obligation. Repeated unapproved overtime
is a performance matter, handled under `POL-GRIEV-001`, not a reason to
withhold pay.

Paid time off does not count as hours worked when calculating overtime.

## 4. Timekeeping

Non-exempt employees must record actual start, end, and unpaid meal times each
workday, and submit their timesheet by 10:00 local time on the first business
day after the pay period closes. Managers approve by end of that day.

A late timesheet does not delay pay. Payroll will pay the employee's scheduled
hours and reconcile any difference in the following period.

## 5. Deductions

### 5.1 Standard Deductions

Statutory deductions (federal, state, and local taxes, Social Security, and
Medicare) are withheld as required. Benefits deductions — medical, dental,
vision, HSA, FSA, and retirement contributions — are taken per the employee's
elections recorded under `POL-BEN-001`.

### 5.2 Garnishments

Helios complies with valid court-ordered garnishments and child support orders.
Affected employees are notified in writing before the first deduction. Payroll
staff must not discuss a garnishment with anyone other than the employee and
the issuing authority.

## 6. Payslips, Errors, and Corrections

Payslips are available in the payroll system within 24 hours of each payday and
are retained for seven years.

### 6.1 Underpayment

If an employee is underpaid, Helios issues an off-cycle correction. For a
shortfall of **more than 10% of expected net pay**, the correction is issued
within **two business days** of the error being confirmed. Smaller shortfalls
are corrected in the next scheduled payroll unless the employee requests
otherwise.

### 6.2 Overpayment

Overpayments must be repaid. Helios will agree a repayment schedule in writing
before recovering any amount, and will not recover more than **15% of net pay**
in a single period without the employee's written agreement. Where an
overpayment is discovered more than 24 months after it occurred, Helios will
generally write it off rather than pursue recovery.

## 7. Pay Changes

Pay changes take effect at the start of a pay period, never mid-period. A
promotion approved on the 20th of a month therefore takes effect on the 1st of
the following month, and the employee is paid at the old rate for the remainder
of the current period.

Retroactive pay adjustments are permitted where the delay was caused by Helios
(for example, a late approval), and are paid as a separate line on the next
payslip.

## 8. Final Pay

On termination, Helios issues final pay in accordance with the law of the
employee's work state, which in several states requires payment on the final
day of employment. Final pay includes:

- all base pay through the last day worked;
- payout of accrued, unused PTO as determined by `POL-PTO-001` §6;
- any earned but unpaid variable pay per §3.2 above.

Floating holidays are not paid out. Company equipment must be returned per
`POL-EQUIP-001`; the value of unreturned equipment is **not** deducted from
final pay, and is pursued separately.

## 9. Pay Confidentiality

Employees may discuss their own pay with anyone, including colleagues. Helios
will not retaliate against an employee for doing so, and any policy or manager
instruction to the contrary is void.

Employees with access to *other* people's pay data — managers, People
Operations, Finance — must treat it as confidential under `POL-SEC-001` and may
discuss it only with those who need it to do their jobs.

## 10. Questions

Payroll questions go to payroll@helios.example via an HR ticket. Questions
about how a compensation decision was reached go to the employee's manager or
People Operations partner.
