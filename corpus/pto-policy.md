# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Helios Systems, Inc. ("Helios") provides paid time off so that employees can
rest, attend to personal matters, and return to work refreshed. This policy
describes how PTO is accrued, requested, approved, and paid out.

This policy applies to all US-based employees. Contractors and interns are not
eligible for PTO; see `POL-BEN-001` (Benefits and Eligibility) for the
definitions of employment type. Statutory leave — including parental leave and
serious-health-condition leave — is governed separately by `POL-LEAVE-002`
(Parental and Medical Leave) and is **not** deducted from the PTO balance.

## 2. Accrual

### 2.1 Accrual Rates

PTO accrues on a per-pay-period basis (24 pay periods per year). Accrual rate is
determined by employment type and completed years of service:

| Employment type | Years of service | Annual accrual | Per pay period |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full-time (>= 30 hrs/week) | 0 – 2 years | 15 days (120 hrs) | 5.00 hrs |
| Full-time | 3 – 5 years | 20 days (160 hrs) | 6.67 hrs |
| Full-time | 6+ years | 25 days (200 hrs) | 8.33 hrs |
| Part-time (20 – 29 hrs/week) | Any | Pro-rated at 0.5x full-time | 2.50 hrs |
| Temporary / Intern | Any | Not eligible | — |

Years of service are measured from the employee's `hire_date`. An employee whose
service anniversary falls mid-year moves to the higher accrual rate on the first
pay period following the anniversary.

### 2.2 Accrual Cap

PTO accrual stops once an employee's balance reaches **1.5x their annual accrual
rate**. Accrual resumes automatically once the balance falls below the cap.
Helios does not retroactively grant PTO that was not accrued because of the cap.

### 2.3 Carryover

Employees may carry over a maximum of **10 days (80 hours)** into the next
calendar year. Balances above 10 days as of December 31 are forfeited, except
where state law requires otherwise (see Section 8).

## 3. Requesting PTO

### 3.1 Notice Requirements

Advance notice requirements scale with the length of the absence:

| Length of absence | Minimum advance notice |
| --- | --- |
| 1 – 2 consecutive business days | 3 business days |
| 3 – 5 consecutive business days | 10 business days |
| 6 – 10 consecutive business days | 20 business days |
| More than 10 consecutive business days | 30 business days, plus skip-level approval |

Requests submitted with less than the minimum notice are not automatically
denied, but require documented manager approval and a stated business
justification.

### 3.2 Approval Workflow

1. The employee submits a PTO request in Helios Workday, specifying start date,
   end date, and total hours.
2. The request routes to the employee's direct manager of record.
3. The manager approves or denies within **3 business days**. If a manager does
   not respond within 3 business days, the request escalates automatically to
   the skip-level manager.
4. Approved requests are deducted from the PTO balance on the first day of the
   absence, not at the time of approval.

Employees must not book non-refundable travel before receiving written approval.

### 3.3 Insufficient Balance

An employee may not take PTO they have not accrued. If a requested absence
exceeds the available balance, the employee may:

- reduce the length of the absence to fit the available balance;
- request unpaid personal leave, which requires skip-level approval; or
- apply an unused floating holiday (see `POL-HOL-001`, Section 3).

Negative PTO balances are not permitted under any circumstances.

## 4. Blackout Periods

To protect delivery and customer commitments, the following are blackout
periods during which PTO requests of **3 or more consecutive days** are
generally denied:

- **Fiscal close:** the last 5 business days and first 3 business days of each
  fiscal quarter (fiscal quarters end March 31, June 30, September 30,
  December 31).
- **Annual customer conference:** the week of the Helios Forum conference
  (September 14 – 18, 2026).
- **Peak support season (Customer Support org only):** November 15 – January 5.

Exceptions to a blackout period require VP-level approval. Blackout periods do
not apply to statutory leave under `POL-LEAVE-002`, to bereavement leave, or to
jury duty.

## 5. Team Coverage Rule

No more than **25% of a team** may be on PTO simultaneously. Where 25% of the
team is fewer than one person, at least one person may still take PTO. Managers
resolve competing requests for the same dates in the order the requests were
submitted.

## 6. Holidays Falling Within PTO

Company-observed holidays that fall within an approved PTO absence are **not**
deducted from the PTO balance. See `POL-HOL-001` for the observed holiday
schedule.

## 7. Sick Time

Helios operates a combined PTO bank; there is no separate sick-time balance for
absences of 1 – 3 days. Absences of **4 or more consecutive days** due to the
employee's own serious health condition are handled under `POL-LEAVE-002`
(Parental and Medical Leave) and are not charged to PTO.

Employees should notify their manager as early as possible on the first day of
an unplanned sick absence. No advance notice is required for sick absences.

## 8. Separation and Payout

Accrued, unused PTO is paid out at separation at the employee's base rate of pay
in states where payout is legally required, including California, Colorado, and
Illinois. In all other states, Helios pays out accrued, unused PTO up to a
maximum of **10 days (80 hours)**.

Employees must submit final PTO requests before their last day of employment.
PTO may not be used to extend a separation date.

## 9. Working While on PTO

PTO is intended for genuine disconnection. Employees on PTO are not expected to
monitor email or respond to messages. Managers must not require work during
approved PTO. An employee who is recalled to work during approved PTO has the
corresponding hours credited back to their balance.

Working remotely from another location is **not** PTO and must instead follow
`POL-REMOTE-001` (Remote and Hybrid Work Policy).

## 10. Policy Questions and Exceptions

Questions about this policy should be directed to People Operations at
`people-ops@helios.example.com`. Exceptions must be approved in writing by the
VP of People Operations and are documented in the employee's record.
