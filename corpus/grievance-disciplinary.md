# Grievance, Investigation, and Disciplinary Procedure

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This procedure describes how Helios Systems, Inc. ("Helios") handles a concern
once it has been raised: how it is triaged, investigated, and concluded, and
what happens when misconduct is substantiated.

`POL-CONDUCT-001` (Code of Conduct) defines what conduct is expected and where
to report a breach. This document is the machinery behind that: it applies once
a report exists. It applies to all workers, including contractors and interns,
though the disciplinary outcomes in §7 apply only to employees.

Performance shortfalls are not misconduct and are handled under `POL-PERF-001`.
The distinction matters: performance is about capability, conduct is about
choice. A single incident can raise both, in which case the conduct strand runs
first.

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Reporter | The person raising the concern |
| Respondent | The person whose conduct is being examined |
| Investigator | The person appointed to establish the facts |
| Decision-maker | The person who decides the outcome; never the investigator |

Separating investigator from decision-maker is deliberate. It keeps the person
who gathered the evidence from also judging it.

## 3. Raising a Concern

Concerns may be raised through any of the following. No channel is a
prerequisite for another, and an employee is never required to raise a matter
with their own manager first:

- the employee's manager, or any other manager;
- their People Operations partner;
- the confidential reporting line, which accepts anonymous reports;
- for legal or financial matters, the General Counsel.

### 3.1 Anonymous Reports

Anonymous reports are investigated. Helios will not attempt to identify an
anonymous reporter. Anonymity does limit what can be established — an
investigator cannot ask a reporter follow-up questions — and the outcome may
therefore be inconclusive where the evidence depends on the reporter's account.

### 3.2 Timeliness

Concerns should be raised within **12 months** of the events. Helios will still
consider older reports, and applies no time limit at all to reports of
harassment, discrimination, safety risk, or unlawful conduct.

## 4. Intake and Triage

People Operations acknowledges every report within **two business days** and
completes triage within **five business days**. Triage decides one of:

| Outcome of triage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Informal resolution | Addressed by conversation or coaching, with consent of the reporter |
| Formal investigation | Proceeds to §5 |
| Referred | Belongs to another process (performance, payroll, safety) |
| No further action | The matter, if fully proven, would not breach any policy |

A reporter who disagrees with the triage outcome may ask for it to be reviewed
by a People Operations lead not involved in the original decision.

Allegations of harassment, discrimination, retaliation, violence, or serious
safety risk always proceed to formal investigation. They are not eligible for
informal resolution, because informal resolution depends on a rough equality
between the parties that these allegations by their nature call into question.

## 5. Investigation

### 5.1 Appointment

The investigator must have no reporting relationship to either party and no
material stake in the outcome. Where no suitable internal investigator exists,
Helios appoints an external one.

### 5.2 Process

The investigator will:

1. Confirm the specific allegations in writing to the respondent.
2. Interview the reporter, the respondent, and relevant witnesses.
3. Collect documentary evidence, including systems records where proportionate.
4. Produce a written report of findings of fact.

The respondent is told what is alleged against them and is given a fair
opportunity to answer it before any finding is made. They may be accompanied by
a colleague at any investigatory meeting.

### 5.3 Timescales

Investigations conclude within **20 business days** of appointment. Where an
investigation will exceed this, both parties are told in writing, with a reason
and a revised date. Delay without explanation is itself a failure of this
procedure.

### 5.4 Standard of Proof

Findings are made on the **balance of probabilities** — whether the conduct is
more likely than not to have occurred. This is not a criminal standard, and
Helios does not require proof beyond reasonable doubt to act.

## 6. Interim Measures

While an investigation is open, Helios may adjust reporting lines, change work
arrangements, or suspend the respondent on full pay.

Interim measures are **neutral**: they are not a sanction, do not imply a
finding, and must not be described as one. Where practical, the burden of an
interim measure falls on the respondent rather than the reporter — moving the
reporter's desk or team in response to their own report is not an acceptable
default.

## 7. Disciplinary Outcomes

Where misconduct is substantiated, the decision-maker selects a proportionate
outcome:

| Stage | Typical trigger | Record retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coaching conversation | Minor, first occurrence | Not on file |
| Written warning | Repeated minor, or moderate first | 12 months |
| Final written warning | Serious, or repeat after written warning | 24 months |
| Dismissal | Gross misconduct, or repeat after final warning | Permanent |

Stages need not be applied in sequence. Gross misconduct — including violence,
theft, deliberate breach of `POL-SEC-001`, falsification of records, and
harassment — may result in dismissal at first occurrence.

Expired warnings are disregarded in later decisions and are removed from the
employee's active record.

## 8. Communication of Outcome

Both parties are told, in writing, whether the allegations were substantiated.
The reporter is told that action has been taken where it has, but is not told
the specific sanction, which remains confidential to the respondent.

## 9. Appeals

Either party may appeal within **10 business days** of the written outcome, on
the grounds that the finding was unsupported by the evidence, the procedure was
unfair, or the sanction was disproportionate.

Appeals are heard by someone senior to, and not involved with, the original
decision-maker. An appeal may confirm or reduce a sanction; it may not increase
one.

## 10. Non-Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, or who
participates in an investigation, is itself gross misconduct under §7.

A concern that turns out to be unfounded is not bad faith. Helios acts only
where a report was knowingly false.

## 11. Records

Investigation records are held by People Operations, separately from the
personnel file, with access restricted to those involved in the decision. They
are retained for six years after conclusion.
