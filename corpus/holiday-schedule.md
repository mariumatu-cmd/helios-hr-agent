# Holiday Schedule and Observance Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy lists the holidays Helios Systems observes, explains how holidays
are applied across office locations, and describes floating holidays.

## 3. Floating Holidays

In addition to the observed holidays above, each full-time employee receives
**two (2) floating holidays** per calendar year, credited on January 1.

- Floating holidays may be used for any purpose, including religious or cultural
  observances not on the company schedule.
- Floating holidays require the same advance notice as a 1 – 2 day PTO request
  (3 business days; see `POL-PTO-001`, Section 3.1).
- Floating holidays **do not carry over** and are forfeited if unused by
  December 31.
- Floating holidays are **not paid out** at separation.
- Employees hired after July 1 receive one (1) floating holiday for that year.

Part-time employees receive one (1) floating holiday per calendar year.

## 4. Weekend Observance

When a US holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday.
When it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday. The observed
date, not the calendar date, is the paid day off.

## 5. Holidays and Location

Employees observe the holiday schedule of their **assigned office location** as
recorded in the HR system, not the schedule of the location they happen to be
working from on a given day. An employee temporarily working from another
country under `POL-INTL-001` continues to observe their home-location schedule.

Employees whose work requires coverage on a holiday (for example, on-call
support) receive an alternate day off, scheduled with their manager within 60
days of the holiday.

## 6. Holidays During Leave

Company holidays that fall during a period of statutory leave under
`POL-LEAVE-002` are treated according to that policy and are not separately
credited.
