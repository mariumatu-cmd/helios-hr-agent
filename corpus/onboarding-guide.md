# New Hire Onboarding Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide sets out what happens before a new employee's first day, during their
first week, and across their first 90 days, and who is responsible for each
step.

## 2. Before Day One (Manager and IT)

Responsibilities in the week before the start date:

| Owner | Task | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiting | Signed offer and background check cleared | 5 business days before start |
| People Ops | Create employee record, assign employee ID | 5 business days before start |
| IT | Order standard equipment bundle per `POL-EQUIP-001` §2 | 10 business days before start |
| IT | Ship equipment to registered address | 3 business days before start |
| IT | Create accounts: email, SSO, chat, HR system | 2 business days before start |
| Manager | Assign an onboarding buddy | 5 business days before start |
| Manager | Draft the 30/60/90 plan | 2 business days before start |
| Manager | Send a welcome note and week-one calendar | 1 business day before start |

Equipment that has not arrived by day one is escalated to `it-help@helios.example.com`;
the employee should not purchase a replacement personally
(`POL-EQUIP-001` §4.2).

## 3. Day One

1. **09:00 – 10:00** — Welcome session with People Operations (live, weekly
   cohort).
2. **10:00 – 11:00** — Device setup, MDM enrolment, MFA and hardware security
   key registration (`POL-SEC-001` §5).
3. **11:00 – 12:00** — Security and acceptable-use training. **Mandatory;** must
   be completed before access to Confidential systems is granted.
4. **13:00 – 13:30** — Manager 1:1.
5. **14:00 – 14:30** — Onboarding buddy introduction.
6. **15:00 – 16:00** — Team introduction and tooling walkthrough.

## 4. Week One Checklist (Employee)

- [ ] Complete I-9 / right-to-work verification — **required within 3 business
      days of the start date by law; this deadline cannot be extended**
- [ ] Complete tax withholding forms
- [ ] Set up direct deposit
- [ ] Complete Security and Acceptable Use training
- [ ] Complete Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment training
- [ ] Enrol hardware security key and authenticator app
- [ ] Register home work address in the HR system
- [ ] Confirm work arrangement (onsite / hybrid / remote) with manager
- [ ] Add emergency contact
- [ ] Submit home-office stipend claim if remote or hybrid
      (`POL-BEN-001` §8)
- [ ] Read `POL-PTO-001`, `POL-SEC-001`, and `POL-CONDUCT-001`

## 5. First 30 Days

**Goal: learn the context.**

- Complete all mandatory compliance training (security, conduct, privacy).
- Meet every member of the immediate team 1:1.
- Meet at least three partners outside the team.
- Ship one small, low-risk change, contribution, or deliverable.
- Agree the 30/60/90 plan in writing with the manager.
- **Benefits enrolment deadline: within 30 days of the start date.** Elections
  made after 30 days cannot be accepted until the next open enrolment or a
  qualifying life event (`POL-BEN-001` §7.2).

## 6. First 60 Days

**Goal: contribute independently.**

- Own a defined workstream or account.
- Complete role-specific training and any required certifications.
- Give and receive first structured feedback with the manager.
- Shadow a customer interaction or, for corporate roles, a partner review.

## 7. First 90 Days

**Goal: operate at role level.**

- Deliver an agreed 90-day outcome.
- Complete a 90-day check-in with the manager and the HR Business Partner.
- Set goals for the next review cycle under `POL-PERF-001`.
- Confirm the onboarding buddy relationship has concluded or continues by
  mutual agreement.

## 8. Key Early Milestones and Deadlines

| Milestone | Timing | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I-9 / right-to-work verification | Within 3 business days | Legal requirement |
| Security training | Day one | `POL-SEC-001` |
| Benefits enrolment | Within 30 days | `POL-BEN-001` §7.2 |
| Medical coverage begins | First of the month after 30 days | `POL-BEN-001` §4 |
| 401(k) deferrals available | After 60 days | `POL-BEN-001` §4 |
| PTO available to use | Accrues from day one; may be used as accrued | `POL-PTO-001` §2 |
| Eligible to request fully remote status | After 6 months | `POL-REMOTE-001` §3 |
| 401(k) company match begins | After 12 months | `POL-BEN-001` §4 |
| Parental leave eligibility | After 12 months | `POL-LEAVE-002` §2.2 |
| First formal performance review | Next cycle after 90 days | `POL-PERF-001` §2 |

## 9. Onboarding Buddy

Every new hire is assigned a buddy from outside their direct reporting line for
the first 90 days. The buddy is a peer guide for informal questions and is not
an evaluator. Buddies do not contribute to performance reviews.

## 10. Manager Responsibilities Through 90 Days

- Weekly 1:1 for the first 8 weeks, then at the standard cadence.
- Written 30/60/90 plan agreed by the end of week two.
- Explicit feedback at 30, 60, and 90 days.
- Confirm the employee has completed all mandatory training by day 30.
