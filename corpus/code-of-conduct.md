# Code of Conduct, Anti-Harassment, and Reporting Policy

## 1. Purpose and Commitment

Helios Systems is committed to a workplace free from harassment, discrimination,
retaliation, and violence. This policy applies to all workers, in every location,
and to all work-related settings including offices, remote work, business travel,
company events, and work-related digital channels.

## 2. Expected Conduct

All workers are expected to:

- treat colleagues, customers, candidates, and partners with respect;
- communicate honestly and raise concerns early;
- comply with all Helios policies and applicable law;
- avoid and disclose conflicts of interest; and
- safeguard Helios and customer information under `POL-SEC-001`.

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

### 3.1 Discrimination

Adverse treatment based on a protected characteristic — including race, colour,
national origin, ancestry, religion, sex, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender
identity or expression, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status,
marital status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law — is
prohibited in every employment decision.

### 3.2 Harassment

Unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic that creates an
intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment, or that is made a
condition of employment. This includes, without limitation: slurs, derogatory
jokes, mockery, display of offensive images, and repeated unwanted contact.

### 3.3 Sexual Harassment

Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favours, and other verbal,
visual, or physical conduct of a sexual nature. This includes *quid pro quo*
harassment, where a term of employment is conditioned on submission to such
conduct, and hostile-environment harassment.

### 3.4 Bullying and Abusive Conduct

Repeated conduct a reasonable person would find hostile or intimidating,
including verbal abuse, deliberate humiliation, sabotage of a colleague's work,
and exclusion intended to harm.

### 3.5 Retaliation

Any adverse action against a person because they reported a concern in good
faith, participated in an investigation, or refused to participate in prohibited
conduct. **Retaliation is prohibited even where the original report is not
substantiated.** Retaliation is treated as seriously as the underlying conduct.

### 3.6 Workplace Violence

Threats, intimidation, physical aggression, and possession of weapons on Helios
premises are prohibited. Threats of imminent harm are an emergency: contact
local emergency services first, then Helios Security at +1-555-0100.

## 4. Reporting Channels

A worker may report a concern through any of the following. There is no
requirement to report to a direct manager, and no requirement to raise the issue
with the other person first.

| Channel | Contact | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct manager | — | Manager must escalate to HR within 24 hours |
| Skip-level manager | — | Where the concern involves the direct manager |
| HR Business Partner | Named in the HR system | |

Counsel and the Audit Committee of the Board.

## 5. Manager Obligations

A manager who receives a report of harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or
violence — or who observes such conduct — **must** escalate it to People
Operations or Legal within **24 hours**. Managers may not:

- investigate a harassment or discrimination allegation themselves;
- promise confidentiality beyond what this policy provides;
- discourage a worker from reporting; or
- discuss the report with anyone who does not have a need to know.

Failure to escalate is itself a violation of this policy.

## 6. Investigation Process

1. **Intake** — People Operations or Legal acknowledges the report within
   **2 business days**.
2. **Assessment** — a determination is made about scope, interim measures, and
   whether outside counsel or an external investigator is required. Interim
   measures may include a schedule change or reporting-line change, and must not
   disadvantage the reporting party.
3. **Investigation** — interviews with the reporting party, the responding
   party, and witnesses; review of relevant records. Investigations are
   conducted by trained investigators.
4. **Findings** — a determination is made on a preponderance-of-the-evidence
   standard.
5. **Outcome** — both parties are informed of the outcome and of any action
   affecting them. Target time from intake to outcome is **30 calendar days**;
   complex matters may take longer, and the reporting party receives a status
   update at least every 14 days.

## 7. Confidentiality

Reports are handled with as much confidentiality as is possible while still
conducting a thorough investigation. Information is shared strictly on a
need-to-know basis. Helios cannot promise complete anonymity where the identity
of the reporting party is necessary to investigate, but an anonymous report may
be made through the Ethics Hotline.

## 8. Consequences

Substantiated violations result in action proportionate to the conduct,
including coaching, written warning, mandatory training, reassignment, demotion,
loss of bonus or equity, or termination. Knowingly making a false report in bad
faith is itself a violation; a good-faith report that is not substantiated is
never a violation.

## 9. Conflicts of Interest

Workers must disclose any situation where personal interests could conflict with
Helios's interests, including:

- a romantic relationship within a direct or indirect reporting line — this must
  be disclosed to People Operations, which will arrange a reporting-line change;
- outside employment, advisory roles, or board seats;
- a financial interest in a Helios customer, supplier, or competitor; and
- hiring or managing a family member or close personal contact.

Disclosure is made to `people-ops@helios.example.com`. Disclosure is not itself
a violation; failure to disclose is.

## 10. Gifts and Anti-Corruption

Workers may not offer or accept anything of value intended to improperly
influence a business decision. Gifts and hospitality with a value over **$100**
must be declined or disclosed. Facilitation payments are prohibited without
exception. Anything involving a government official requires prior Legal
approval.

## 11. Support Resources

1-555-0177 and is independent of Helios management. Using the EAP is never
reported to a manager.
