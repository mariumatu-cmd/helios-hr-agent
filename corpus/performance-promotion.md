# Performance Management and Promotion Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how performance is set, reviewed, and rated at Helios
Systems, and how promotion and compensation decisions are made.

## 2. Review Cycle

Helios runs **two review cycles per year**:

| Cycle | Period covered | Review window | Outcomes effective |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | January – June | July 1 – July 31 | August 1 |
| H2 | July – December | January 5 – January 31 | February 1 |

Employees who joined fewer than **90 days** before the start of a review window
receive a written check-in instead of a formal rating and are not eligible for a
merit increase in that cycle.

## 3. Performance Ratings

| Rating | Meaning | Approx. distribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Exceptional** | Consistently exceeded role expectations with outsized impact | ~10% |
| **Exceeds Expectations** | Delivered above role expectations | ~25% |
| **Meets Expectations** | Delivered the role well and reliably | ~55% |
| **Partially Meets** | Fell short in one or more significant areas | ~8% |
| **Does Not Meet** | Substantially failed to deliver role expectations | ~2% |

Distributions are guidance for calibration, not quotas. A manager is never
required to assign a low rating to fit a distribution.

**"Meets Expectations" is a good rating.** It is the expected outcome for a
strong performer delivering their role, and it satisfies the performance
requirement for fully remote eligibility under `POL-REMOTE-001` §3.

## 4. Inputs to a Review

- Employee self-assessment.
- Manager assessment against the role's level expectations in the job
  architecture.
- Peer feedback from at least two colleagues, selected jointly by the employee
  and manager.
- Goal outcomes agreed at the start of the cycle.

Reviews assess **what** was delivered and **how** it was delivered. Conduct
inconsistent with `POL-CONDUCT-001` caps the rating at "Partially Meets"
regardless of delivery.

## 5. Calibration

Ratings are calibrated in a group session at the department level before being
shared with employees, to reduce inconsistency between managers. Calibration
notes are confidential. An employee may ask their manager for the rationale
behind their rating at any time.

## 6. Compensation Outcomes

| Rating | Typical merit increase | Equity refresh | Bonus multiplier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exceptional | 6 – 10% | Yes, above target | 1.3 – 1.5x |
| Exceeds Expectations | 4 – 6% | Yes, at target | 1.1 – 1.3x |
| Meets Expectations | 2 – 4% | Yes, at target | 1.0x |
| Partially Meets | 0 – 2% | No | 0.5x |
| Does Not Meet | 0% | No | 0x |

Merit increases are effective on the cycle dates in Section 2. Compensation is
also reviewed against market benchmarks annually; a market adjustment may be
made outside the merit process.

## 7. Promotion

### 7.1 Criteria

Promotion recognises sustained performance **at the next level**, not
exceptional performance at the current level. The criteria are:

1. Demonstrated operation at the next level's scope for at least **two
   consecutive review cycles**.
2. A rating of "Exceeds Expectations" or higher in the most recent cycle.
3. A written promotion case prepared by the manager, evidencing next-level
   scope, impact, and behaviours.
4. Endorsement in the department calibration session.
5. Approval by the skip-level manager and the HR Business Partner.

### 7.2 Timing

Promotions are decided within the two review cycles. Off-cycle promotions are
permitted only where a role has materially changed, and require VP approval.

### 7.3 Time in Role

There is no minimum time in role, but the two-cycle evidence requirement in
Section 7.1 means promotion typically occurs after at least 12 months at a
level.

## 8. Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)

### 8.1 When a PIP Is Used

A PIP is used where performance is rated "Partially Meets" or "Does Not Meet",
or where a significant performance concern arises between cycles. A PIP must be
preceded by documented, specific feedback and a reasonable opportunity to
improve.

### 8.2 Structure

- Duration: **60 days**, extendable once by 30 days.
- Contains specific, measurable objectives and a weekly check-in cadence.
- Reviewed and approved by the HR Business Partner before it is issued.
- The employee may request a support person at PIP meetings.

### 8.3 Consequences During a PIP

While on an active PIP an employee is **not eligible** for:

- promotion;
- a merit increase or equity refresh;
- an internal transfer; or
- a change to fully remote work arrangement (`POL-REMOTE-001` §3.3).

Successful completion of a PIP restores eligibility immediately. A PIP is not a
prelude to a predetermined outcome; the majority of Helios PIPs conclude
successfully.

## 9. Internal Transfers

Employees may apply for an internal role after **12 months** in their current
role, provided they are not on an active PIP and their most recent rating is
"Meets Expectations" or higher. The current manager is notified when the
employee reaches the interview stage, not at application.

## 10. Disagreeing With a Review

An employee who disagrees with a rating may:

1. discuss it with their manager and add a written employee comment to the
   review record, which is retained permanently;
2. raise it with their HR Business Partner; and
3. request a review by the skip-level manager within **14 days** of receiving
   the rating.

Raising a concern about a review is protected activity; retaliation for doing so
violates `POL-CONDUCT-001` §3.5.
