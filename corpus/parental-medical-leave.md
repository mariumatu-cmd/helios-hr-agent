# Parental and Medical Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes leave available for the birth or placement of a child, for
an employee's own serious health condition, and for the care of a family member
with a serious health condition. Leave under this policy is **separate from PTO**
and is not deducted from the PTO balance (`POL-PTO-001`, Section 1).

## 2. Paid Parental Leave

### 2.1 Entitlement

| Role in the birth or placement | Paid leave |
| --- | --- |
| Birthing parent | **16 weeks** at 100% of base pay |
| Non-birthing parent, including adoptive and foster | **8 weeks** at 100% of base pay |

Leave applies equally regardless of gender, marital status, or the manner in
which the employee became a parent, including birth, adoption, surrogacy, and
foster placement.

### 2.2 Eligibility

- **12 months of continuous service** at the date leave begins
  (`POL-BEN-001`, Section 4).
- Full-time employees receive the full entitlement. Part-time employees receive
  a pro-rated entitlement based on scheduled hours.
- Temporary employees, interns, and contractors are not eligible.

### 2.3 Timing and Structure

- Leave must be taken within **12 months** of the birth or placement.
- Leave may be taken in up to **three separate blocks**, each of at least two
  consecutive weeks, with manager agreement on the schedule.
- Birthing parents' leave begins on the date of birth unless a medical
  disability leave begins earlier (Section 3).
- Multiple births or placements do not increase the entitlement.
- Both parents may be Helios employees; each receives their own entitlement.

### 2.4 Notice

Provide at least **30 days'** notice where the need is foreseeable. Where it is
not foreseeable — for example premature birth or an expedited placement —
notify People Operations as soon as practicable.

## 3. Medical Leave (Employee's Own Condition)

### 3.1 When It Applies

Absences of **four (4) or more consecutive days** due to the employee's own
serious health condition are handled under this policy rather than PTO
(`POL-PTO-001`, Section 7). Absences of one to three days are ordinary sick
absences charged to PTO.

### 3.2 Short-Term Disability

Full-time employees are covered by employer-paid short-term disability:

| Period | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Days 1 – 7 | Elimination period; employee may use PTO or take unpaid |
| Week 2 – week 8 | 100% of base pay |
| Week 9 – week 26 | 60% of base pay |

Part-time employees are not covered by short-term disability
(`POL-BEN-001`, Section 3) and should contact People Operations to discuss state
disability programs and unpaid leave options.

### 3.3 Long-Term Disability

Long-term disability begins after 26 weeks of continuous disability and pays 60%
of base pay, subject to the plan document. Full-time employees only.

### 3.4 Medical Certification

Medical certification is required for any medical leave of more than five
consecutive business days. Certification is submitted directly to the
third-party leave administrator, **not** to the employee's manager. Managers
receive only the approved leave dates and never the underlying medical
information.

## 4. Family Care Leave

Employees with 12 months of service may take up to **12 weeks** of leave per
rolling 12-month period to care for a spouse, domestic partner, child, or parent
with a serious health condition. The first **4 weeks** are paid at 100% of base
pay; the remainder is unpaid unless the employee elects to apply accrued PTO.

## 5. Bereavement Leave

Bereavement leave is available immediately on hire and is not charged to PTO:

| Relationship | Paid days |
| --- | --- |
| Spouse, domestic partner, child, or parent | 10 days |
| Sibling, grandparent, grandchild, parent-in-law | 5 days |
| Other close relationship, including chosen family | 3 days |
| Pregnancy loss or failed adoption or surrogacy | 10 days |

Additional unpaid time may be approved by the manager. Travel-related time is
included within the days above.

## 6. Interaction with Other Entitlements

- **Job protection:** Leave under this policy runs concurrently with FMLA and
  with any applicable state leave program where the law permits.
- **Holidays:** Company holidays falling within a leave period are covered by
  the leave benefit and are not separately credited
  (`POL-HOL-001`, Section 6).
- **PTO accrual:** PTO continues to accrue during paid leave. PTO does not
  accrue during unpaid leave beyond 30 days.
- **Benefits:** Medical, dental, and vision coverage continues unchanged during
  approved leave. The employee remains responsible for their premium share.
- **401(k):** Deferrals pause during unpaid leave; the company match resumes on
  return.

## 7. Returning to Work

- Employees return to the same position, or an equivalent position with
  equivalent pay, benefits, and responsibilities.
- Return-to-work dates may be adjusted once, by up to two weeks, with 10
  business days' notice to the manager.
- Birthing parents are entitled to a **phased return**: four weeks at 80%
  schedule and 100% pay, on request.
- Lactation space and reasonable break time are provided at every Helios office
  and must be accommodated for remote employees' schedules.

## 8. Requesting Leave

1. Notify People Operations at `leave@helios.example.com`, ideally 30 days in
   advance.
2. People Operations opens a case with the third-party leave administrator.
3. The administrator contacts the employee directly for any certification.
4. The employee's manager receives only the approved dates.
5. A written leave confirmation is issued before the leave begins.

Employees should not disclose medical details to their manager and managers must
not request them. Doing so is a privacy violation under `POL-CONDUCT-001`.
