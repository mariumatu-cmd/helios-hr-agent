# Benefits and Eligibility Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the benefits Helios Systems offers, who is eligible for
each, and when elections can be made or changed.

## 2. Employment Types

Eligibility for every benefit is determined by employment type as recorded in
the HR system field `employment_type`.

| Employment type | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Full-time** | Regular employee scheduled for 30 or more hours per week |
| **Part-time** | Regular employee scheduled for 20 – 29 hours per week |
| **Temporary** | Employee on a fixed-term contract of 6 months or less |
| **Intern** | Enrolled student in a defined internship program |
| **Contractor** | Engaged through a vendor or as an independent contractor; not a Helios employee |

Contractors are not eligible for any Helios benefit. Questions about contractor
compensation are handled by Procurement, not People Operations.

## 4. Waiting Periods

| Benefit | Coverage begins |
| --- | --- |
| Medical, dental, vision | First day of the month following **30 days** of employment |
| Life, AD&D, disability | First day of the month following **30 days** of employment |
| 401(k) deferral | First payroll following **60 days** of employment |
| 401(k) company match | First payroll following **12 months** of employment |
| Wellness and learning stipends | Immediately on hire |
| Paid parental leave | After **12 months** of continuous service |

An employee who has not yet satisfied a waiting period is **not eligible** for
that benefit, even if they have made an election.

## 5. Medical, Dental, and Vision

Helios offers three medical plans:

| Plan | Type | Employee premium (monthly) | Deductible (individual) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Helios Core | PPO | $95 | $1,500 |
| Helios Plus | PPO | $180 | $750 |
| Helios Saver | HDHP with HSA | $40 | $3,200 |

Helios contributes **$1,000 per year** to the HSA of employees enrolled in the
Helios Saver plan, deposited in equal amounts each pay period.

Dependent coverage is available for a spouse, domestic partner, and children up
to age 26. Dependent verification documentation must be provided within **30
days** of adding a dependent, or coverage for that dependent is cancelled.

## 6. 401(k) Retirement Plan

- Helios matches **100% of the first 3%** of eligible pay deferred, plus
  **50% of the next 2%**, for a maximum match of 4% of eligible pay.
- The match is subject to the 12-month service requirement in Section 4.
- Employer match vests on a **two-year cliff**: an employee who separates before
  completing 24 months of service forfeits the entire unvested match. Employee
  deferrals are always 100% vested.
- Contribution changes may be made at any time and take effect the following
  pay period.

## 7. Open Enrollment and Qualifying Life Events

### 7.1 Open Enrollment

Annual open enrollment runs **November 1 – November 20** each year, with
elections effective January 1. Employees who do not actively elect are enrolled
in the same medical plan as the prior year, except that **HSA and FSA
contributions reset to zero** and must be re-elected every year.

### 7.2 Qualifying Life Events

Outside of open enrollment, elections may only be changed within **30 days** of
a qualifying life event:

- marriage, divorce, or legal separation;
- birth, adoption, or placement of a child;
- death of a dependent;
- a dependent gaining or losing other coverage;
- a spouse's employment change affecting coverage; or
- a change in employment type that affects eligibility.

Requests made more than 30 days after the event **cannot be approved** — this is
an IRS requirement, not a Helios discretion, and People Operations cannot grant
an exception. The employee must wait for the next open enrollment.

## 8. Stipends

| Stipend | Amount | Frequency | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wellness | $75 | Monthly | Gym, fitness apps, therapy, nutrition |
| Learning and development | $2,000 | Annual | Courses, certifications, conferences, books |
| Home office | $750 | One-time on hire | Furniture and ergonomics; see `POL-EQUIP-001` |
| Home office refresh | $300 | Every 3 years | Remote and hybrid employees only |
| Internet | $60 | Monthly | Remote employees only; hybrid employees not eligible |

Stipends are reimbursed through the expense system under `POL-EXP-001` and are
taxable where required by law. Unused stipend amounts do not carry over.

## 9. Changing Employment Type

A change in employment type that affects eligibility is a qualifying life event.
Benefits change on the effective date of the employment-type change, not the
date of the request. An employee moving from full-time to part-time loses
short-term and long-term disability coverage and moves to the employee-only
medical subsidy.

## 10. COBRA and Separation

Medical, dental, and vision coverage ends on the **last day of the month** in
which employment ends. COBRA continuation election materials are sent within 14
days of separation. See `POL-PTO-001` Section 8 for PTO payout at separation.
