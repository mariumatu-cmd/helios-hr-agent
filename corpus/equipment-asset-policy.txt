================================================================================
1. PURPOSE
================================================================================

This policy describes how Helios Systems issues, maintains, refreshes, and
recovers company equipment. It applies to every worker issued a Helios asset,
including contractors and interns.

All Helios equipment remains the property of Helios Systems at all times,
regardless of how long it has been in an employee's possession.

================================================================================

Every new worker receives a standard equipment bundle determined by their role
family. Requests outside the standard bundle require manager approval and are
reviewed by IT Operations.

  Success

Mobile phones are issued only to workers in on-call rotations, Field Sales, and
the executive team. Other workers may claim the monthly phone allowance of $40
instead.

================================================================================
3. REFRESH CYCLE
================================================================================

  Asset                 Refresh interval    Notes
  --------------------  ------------------  ------------------------------
  Laptop                36 months           Measured from asset issue date
  Monitor               60 months           Replaced on failure otherwise
  Headset               24 months
  Mobile phone          24 months
  Keyboard / mouse      On failure          No fixed interval
  Security key          On failure          Replacement requires identity
                                            verification

Refresh is automatic: IT contacts the employee approximately 30 days before the
laptop refresh date to schedule the swap. Employees may defer a refresh once,
by up to 6 months.

Early replacement outside the refresh cycle requires a documented business
justification and Director approval, except for hardware failure, which is
always replaced at no cost to the team's budget.

================================================================================
4. HOME OFFICE EQUIPMENT
================================================================================

4.1 STIPEND

Remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of $750 on
hire, and a refresh stipend of $300 every three years. The stipend is
administered under POL-BEN-001 Section 8 and is claimed through the expense
system.

The stipend covers desks, chairs, lighting, and ergonomic accessories. It does
not cover computing equipment, which IT purchases directly (Section 4.2).

4.2 COMPUTING EQUIPMENT IS NEVER SELF-PURCHASED

Employees must not buy laptops, monitors, peripherals, or software personally
and submit an expense claim. Such claims are denied under POL-EXP-001
Section 8. All computing equipment is ordered by IT Operations and shipped to
the employee's registered address.

4.3 ERGONOMIC ASSESSMENTS

Any employee may request a remote ergonomic assessment at no cost. Equipment
recommended by an ergonomic assessment, or required as a documented workplace
accommodation, is purchased by Helios in addition to the stipend and does not
reduce the stipend balance.

================================================================================
5. DAMAGE, LOSS, AND THEFT
================================================================================

Normal wear and accidental damage are covered by Helios at no cost to the
employee. There is no deductible and no charge-back to the employee or team.

Loss or theft must be reported to IT and to the Security Operations Center
within 4 hours of discovery, per POL-SEC-001 Section 3.3. A police report is
required for theft claims.

Repeated loss - three or more devices lost within a rolling 24-month period -
is reviewed by IT Operations with the employee's manager and People Operations.

Deliberate damage or sale of Helios equipment is treated as misappropriation of
company property under POL-CONDUCT-001.

================================================================================
6. PERSONAL USE
================================================================================

Incidental personal use of Helios equipment is permitted within the limits of
POL-SEC-001 Section 6. Employees must not:

  - allow family members or other third parties to use Helios equipment;
  - store personal media libraries or personal financial records on a Helios
    device;
  - remove MDM enrolment or the endpoint security agent; or
  - install unapproved software.

Employees should assume that anything stored on a Helios device may be reviewed
by Helios where there is a legitimate business or legal reason to do so.

================================================================================
7. TRAVEL AND LOANER DEVICES
================================================================================

Workers travelling to a location on the Elevated Risk list in POL-SEC-001
Section 8 must travel with a clean loaner laptop and loaner phone, requested
from IT with a minimum of 5 business days' notice. The primary device must not
travel to these locations.

Loaner devices are also available for any international trip on request, and are
recommended where the employee will be working from a shared or public space.

Loaner devices must be returned within 5 business days of return travel and are
wiped and re-imaged on receipt.

================================================================================
8. RETURN AT SEPARATION
================================================================================

All Helios equipment must be returned by the employee's last day of employment.
IT sends a prepaid shipping kit to remote employees approximately 7 days before
the separation date.

  Asset                     Return required
  ------------------------  ----------------------------------------
  Laptop                    Yes
  Monitor(s)                Yes
  Mobile phone (issued)     Yes
  Security key              Yes
  Headset                   Yes
  Docking station, hub      Yes
  Keyboard, mouse           No - employee may retain
  Laptop stand              No - employee may retain
  Home office furniture     No - purchased with stipend, employee
                            retains ownership

Equipment not returned within 21 days of the separation date is invoiced to the
former employee at fair market value, and where permitted by state law, may be
deducted from final pay.

Employees must not wipe, reset, or factory-restore a device before returning it;
IT performs a forensic-safe wipe after data-retention checks.

================================================================================
9. ACCESSIBILITY AND ACCOMMODATIONS
================================================================================

Equipment required as a reasonable accommodation is provided by Helios at no
cost to the employee or the team budget, and is not subject to the standard
issue limits in Section 2. Accommodation requests are handled confidentially by
People Operations; IT is informed only of the equipment to be provided, never of
the underlying medical information.
