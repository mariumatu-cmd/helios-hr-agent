# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose and Principles

Helios Systems reimburses reasonable, necessary, and documented expenses
incurred in the course of business. Employees should spend Helios money as
carefully as their own, choose the lower-cost reasonable option, and submit
accurate claims promptly.

## 2. Submission Requirements

### 2.1 Deadlines

Expenses must be submitted within **60 calendar days** of being incurred.
Claims submitted after 60 days require Director approval, and claims submitted
after **120 days cannot be reimbursed** under any circumstances.

### 2.2 Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every expense of **$25 or more**. A credit
card statement line is not an itemised receipt. Expenses under $25 may be
claimed without a receipt, up to $150 per expense report.

Receipts must show the vendor, date, itemised amounts, and total. For meals with
other people, the claim must also list the attendees and the business purpose.

### 2.3 Approval Thresholds

| Total expense report amount | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $500 | Direct manager |
| $501 – $2,500 | Direct manager + Director |
| $2,501 – $10,000 | Director + VP |
| Over $10,000 | VP + Finance Business Partner |

Pre-approval is required for any single expense over $2,500 and for all
international business travel.

## 3. Air Travel

- Book through the Helios travel portal (Navan). Bookings outside the portal
  require documented justification and are reimbursed at the lower of actual
  cost or the portal fare for the same itinerary.
- **Economy class** is required for all flights with a scheduled duration under
  **6 hours**.
- **Premium economy** is permitted for flights of 6 hours or more.
- **Business class** requires VP approval and is permitted only for flights of
  **10 hours or more**.
- Book at least **14 days** in advance where the itinerary is known.
- Seat selection up to $75 per segment and one checked bag are reimbursable.
- Travel upgrades purchased with personal funds or points are not reimbursable.

## 4. Lodging

- Reimbursable up to **$300 per night** in Tier 1 cities (New York, San
  Francisco, London, Tokyo, Singapore, Zurich) and **$200 per night** elsewhere,
  excluding taxes and mandatory fees.
- Short-term rentals are permitted at or below the applicable nightly cap.
- Room-service and minibar charges are reimbursable only within the meal per
  diem in Section 5.
- In-room movies, spa services, and laundry for trips under 5 nights are not
  reimbursable.

## 5. Meals and Per Diem

Meals on business travel are reimbursed on a **per diem** basis, not by actual
receipt, except for business meals with customers or candidates.

| Location tier | Daily per diem |
| --- | --- |
| Tier 1 cities | $85 |
| United States, other | $65 |
| International, other | $70 |

Partial travel days are reimbursed at 75% of the per diem. Meals provided by the
company or included in a conference fee reduce the per diem for that day by:
breakfast $15, lunch $25, dinner $40.

Business meals with customers, partners, or candidates are reimbursed at actual
cost with an itemised receipt and a documented attendee list. Alcohol is
reimbursable only at customer or candidate meals and must be itemised
separately; alcohol is never reimbursable on a per-diem day.

## 6. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursable:

- traffic and parking fines, and vehicle towing;
- personal entertainment, including in-flight entertainment purchases;
- travel insurance purchased separately (Helios provides business travel
  insurance automatically);
- costs for spouses, partners, family members, or pets travelling with the
  employee;
- home internet, except the remote-employee internet stipend under
  `POL-BEN-001` Section 8;
- commuting between the employee's home and their assigned Helios office;
- personal grooming, clothing, and toiletries;
- expenses already covered by a stipend under `POL-BEN-001`.

### 6.4 Voluntary Remote Work Abroad

Travel, accommodation, meals, coworking space, and local transport associated
with **voluntary international remote work** under `POL-INTL-001` are **not**
business travel and are **not reimbursable**. Only costs Helios specifically
mandates — for example an Information Security–required loaner device or a
required eSIM data plan — are reimbursable, and must be pre-approved.

## 7. Ground Transportation

- Rideshare and taxi are reimbursable for business travel and for late-night
  travel from the office after 21:00.
- Rental cars are reimbursable at mid-size class or below; premium classes
  require Director approval. Decline the rental company's collision waiver in
  the United States, where Helios corporate insurance applies; accept it
  internationally.
- Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS standard rate,
  measured from the office or from home, whichever is shorter.
- Airport parking is reimbursable for trips of up to 7 days; for longer trips,
  claim the lower of parking or round-trip rideshare.

## 8. Equipment and Software Purchases

Employees must not purchase computing equipment, peripherals, or software
directly and claim reimbursement. All such purchases are made by IT under
`POL-EQUIP-001`. Direct purchases will be denied.

Ergonomic furniture and accessories are handled through the home-office stipend
in `POL-BEN-001` Section 8, not as an expense claim.

## 9. Corporate Card

Employees who travel more than four times per year are issued a Helios corporate
card. Corporate card transactions must still be reconciled in an expense report
within 60 days. Personal use of the corporate card is prohibited; incidental
personal charges must be repaid within 30 days.

## 10. Audit and Misuse

Finance audits a random 10% of expense reports each month, plus all reports over
$5,000. Knowingly submitting a false or inflated claim is treated as a serious
violation under `POL-CONDUCT-001` and may result in termination and recovery of
funds.
